# LimitMark — Public Historical Snapshot

**Snapshot:** 25 November 2026. This is a bounded public summary of a reconstructed project history. The owner's detailed internal memory package was independently reviewed and accepted for historical reconstruction; that acceptance does not qualify software or operations.

## Identity and direction

LimitMark began in September 2026 with a Turkish-language B2B website for manually scoped resilience testing. By November, preserved project instructions described an intended **independent, layered Customer Defense Plane** for protecting legitimate customer activity under adverse conditions. The exact historical discussion that led from the original website brief to this broader direction has not been recovered.

The intended defense is not simply a load-testing product, nor a dependent add-on to a third-party security provider. Provider protection may be additive, but independent LimitMark qualification cannot claim provider decisions as its own. Legitimate-user continuity is the primary success criterion. These statements concern the project's design intent, not a demonstrated general operational capability.

## Engineering tracks

The repository includes a Next.js/React/TypeScript website and supporting inquiry, administration and evidence-review functionality. Its application features and its defense checkpoints follow different development paths; a successful build or CI result on `master` does not establish integration of every defense branch.

The defense research has examined request-shape screening, independent lane/budget decisions, guarded origin boundaries, bounded local checks and scoped field evidence. Logical separation of decision layers does not by itself prove physical or failure-domain isolation.

The approved historical M4 *architectural direction* concerned qualification of a standalone composed defense path with a synthetic origin in a controlled environment. M5 retained a separate, proposed line of future recovery and execution-identity lifecycle work. M4 offline engineering acceptance, operational capability evidence, field authorization and production qualification are distinct states; none follows automatically from another.

## Evidence and decision discipline

Project decisions distinguish explicit authority, historical evidence, bounded local acceptance, externally observed results and unverified inference. A passing test, historical assertion, repeated report or independent AI review does not automatically establish general security qualification. Historical failures are retained rather than retroactively reclassified without evidence.

The reconstructed history also contains unresolved questions, including the exact product pivot rationale, future supervisory-role relationships, commercialization and the final owner selection after two differing strategic recommendations on 25 November. The public summary deliberately does not turn those open questions into a new roadmap.
